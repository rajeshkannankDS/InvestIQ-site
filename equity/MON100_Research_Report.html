1. ETF Structure & Sponsor Metadata

Section 1 — The View (plain English)
  • India's most established Nasdaq-100 wrapper: launched 29-Mar-2011 by Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund (Motilal Oswal Asset Management), listed on NSE (MON100) and BSE (533385), ISIN INF247L01AP3, AMFI scheme code 114984 — 15 years 7 months of live trading.IMPORTANT
  • It is a passive foreign-index fund in ETF clothing: it buys the 100 Nasdaq-100 constituents (excluding financials) in index weights to track the NASDAQ-100 Total Return Index in INR, with Nifty 50 TRI as the secondary benchmark. Every rupee of return therefore = US share prices × USD/INR × (−0.60% fee).IMPORTANT
  • No promoter, no balance sheet to analyse — the metadata questions that matter are cost, tracking, liquidity and currency, which live in sections 2 and 5. Two operational notes: international ETF iNAV is disclosed with a 15-second delay reflecting only currency movement from the previous day's NAV, and direct-with-AMC dealing is creation-size/₹25 Cr territory (market makers exempt) — retail trades on the exchange.
