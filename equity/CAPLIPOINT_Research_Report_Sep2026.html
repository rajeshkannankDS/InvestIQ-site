STANDALONE VERDICT (analyst view, Sep 2026)
ACCUMULATE — QUALITY COMPOUNDER · 12M horizon

Caplin Point is a rare small cap with large-cap economics: 35% operating margins held for 13 consecutive quarters, ~30% net margin, ROE 20.9% / ROCE 24.2%, borrowings of just ₹221 Cr against reserves of ₹3,355 Cr (D/E 0.06x) and interest of ~₹1 Cr a year. Verdict: accumulate in tranches rather than a lump sum, adding only while margins, returns and debt stay where they are.

4. Profitability & Efficiency

Section 4 — The View (plain English)
  • Excellent margins: OPM 35% in FY26 (up from 30% in FY23 and 24% back in 2015) and a net margin of about 30% (₹650 ÷ ₹2,187 Cr) — ₹30 of every ₹100 of sales lands as profit. This is large-cap-quality profitability inside a small cap.IMPORTANT
  • Returns are high but softening: ROE 20.9% and ROCE 24.2%, with ROCE at 26% for FY23–FY25 and 24% in FY26 — versus 68% back in 2015. The company earns well, but each new rupee of capital earns less than the old ones did.IMPORTANT
  • Signature test (Revenue↑ + Margin↑ + ROCE↑): revenue ↑ and margin ↑ both pass, but ROCE ↓ (68% → 24% over the decade) and working capital is ballooning (CCC 173 → 194 days) — the third leg of the quality test is missing.IMPORTANT

  • Cash conversion is the weak spot: debtors now take 138 days (was 98 in FY23) and inventory 181 days — growth is being financed out of the balance sheet rather than out of payables.
