1. Company / Stock Metadata

Section 1 — The View (plain English)
  • Identity and ownership are clean: top credit ratings (AAA/A1+ domestically, Baa1/A− globally), promoter holding steady near 50% for 12 quarters with zero pledge — no borrowing against the family's shares.IMPORTANT
  • Ownership structure is defensive: promoters + Indian institutions together hold ~71.6%, so only ~29% (mostly foreign) is genuinely in play.IMPORTANT

  • Small watch item: retail shareholder count jumped to 46.5 lakh (+2.3 lakh in one quarter) — a large late-entering retail crowd is usually a caution sign, not a bullish one.

3. Growth Metrics

Section 3 — The View (plain English)
  • Use the 3-year numbers, not the 5-year ones: revenue has compounded just 6.4%/year and profit 8.9%/year over 3 years (the flashy 17.8% five-year rate is a COVID-low-base and oil-price artefact).IMPORTANT
  • The earnings down-cycle appears to have ended: Q1 FY27 delivered a record ₹47,517 Cr quarterly EBITDA (+10.7% YoY) and underlying profit growth of +9.3% once you strip last year's one-time Asian Paints gain.IMPORTANT
  • The headline TTM profit fall of −7.9% is a base effect, not deterioration — same quarter last year contained an ₹8,924 Cr one-time gain.
  • Two engines are out of sync: Jio is compounding (revenue +11.8%, EBITDA +15.1%) while Retail is slowing (+7.4% revenue, EBITDA −1.1% as it spends on quick commerce).
  • Growth quality verdict: moderate (score 5.5/10) — the group grows near GDP-plus rates; the excitement has to come from margins and the consumer businesses, not the top line.

4. Profitability & Efficiency

Section 4 — The View (plain English)
  • The weakest section of the report (5.0/10): Reliance earns ROE 8.9% and ROCE 10.3% — barely above what shareholders' money could earn elsewhere, and flat for a decade. A great compounder earns 15%+.IMPORTANT
  • The green-light pattern (revenue rising + margins rising + returns rising together) has not appeared: margins slipped from 18% (FY24) to 17% (FY26) and 15.4% in Q1 FY27.IMPORTANT
  • Reasons are structural, not cosmetic: huge refining/spectrum/capex assets in the denominator, plus heavy current investment in New Energy and 5G that earns nothing yet.
  • Small yellow flag: cash conversion cycle worsened from −8 days to +25 days — customers are taking longer to pay (debtor days up from 12 to 20), though suppliers still largely fund working capital (−66 days).
  • What would upgrade this section: two consecutive quarters of margin expansion plus ROE moving toward 9.5–10%. Until then, cap the valuation multiple you are willing to pay.

5. Balance Sheet / Financial Strength

Section 5 — The View (plain English)
  • The best section of the report (8.5/10): after one year of profits you could repay all net debt 0.6 times over, interest is covered 6.6×, and the group holds ₹2.43 lakh crore of cash and investments.IMPORTANT
  • The debt-cycling pattern is complete: construction-in-progress (money locked in unfinished factories/plants) has fallen two years running — ₹3.39 lakh cr → ₹2.38 lakh cr — meaning the big capex wave is over and deleveraging + buyback/dividend capacity is next.IMPORTANT
  • Balance sheet has funded its own growth: working capital is negative (−66 days) — suppliers and subscribers effectively lend to the business.
  • Domestic ratings are the highest possible (AAA/Stable, A1+); global ratings are investment grade (Moody's Baa1, S&P A−) and were upgraded in FY26.
  • Verify before quoting: contingent liabilities are quoted two ways (₹12,649 Cr vs ₹78,282 Cr including disputed tax demands) — see the flag in Appendix B.

Appendix B — The View

  • Every tag in the report tells you the evidence quality: Fact reported number · Calc derived here · Mgmt company statement · View analyst judgement.

Methodology & definitions Calc

  • Report currency: ₹ Cr unless stated; market data as of 19-Oct-2026 close (fundamentals 01-Oct-2026).
  • Operating Profit (Screener) = Sales − Expenses = EBITDA (validated: Q1 FY27 ₹47,517 Cr matches RIL release). EBIT = OP − Depreciation.
  • EV = Market cap + Net debt (excludes minority interest & current investments; treat EV multiples as approximate).
  • Net debt = Gross borrowings − cash & equivalents (Screener basis ₹1,64,657 − ₹39,940 ≈ values differ from company's ₹1,24,717 Cr net-of-investments figure; both shown wherever material).
  • CAGR = (End/Start)^(1/n) − 1 on FY-end figures; 3Y = FY23→FY26, 5Y = FY21→FY26, 10Y = FY16→FY26.
  • ROE = PAT ÷ average net worth; ROCE = EBIT ÷ (net worth + borrowings) per Screener.
  • FCF = CFO − capex; two capex definitions coexist (Screener derived vs company-reported) — both shown, never mixed.
  • Tags: Fact = reported number · Calc = derived here · Mgmt = company statement · View = analyst judgement.
