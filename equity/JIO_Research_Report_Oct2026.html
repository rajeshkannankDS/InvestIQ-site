1. Metadata & Ownership — Issue Structure, Promoter, Governance

Section 1 — The View (plain English)
  • Who and what: Jio Platforms Ltd (JPL) is Reliance Industries' telecom + digital holding company — the parent of Reliance Jio Infocomm Ltd (RJIL), which runs the Jio network. It is India's largest telecom operator by subscribers and carries about 60% of India's wireless data traffic. The IPO is 100% fresh equity (no promoter selling) with proceeds earmarked mostly to cut RJIL's debt.IMPORTANT
  • Ownership after the issue: Reliance Industries' 66.4% → ~64.4%. Ownership stays with the promoter family (Mukesh Ambani remains Chairman & Managing Director). Free float after listing stays small — roughly 22–24% once RIL's post-issue stake and the 5% employee/IPO quota are netted off (Calc).
  • Issue mechanics you must hold in mind: band ₹1,065–1,119, min lot 12 shares (₹12,780–13,428), ₹2,500 Cr reserved for eligible Reliance Industries shareholders (record date 14-Oct-2026; cap ₹2 lakh/application), quota split 35% retail / 18% NII / 50% QIB (approximately, per RHP), one lot per valid retail application, no discount for retail (the ₹1,065 floor applies to everyone).IMPORTANT
  • Governance: promoter-controlled, big-business group structure; the headline governance issue is related-party revenue — 77.08% of FY26 revenue comes through promoter-owned Reliance Retail as distributor (covered in Section 4). Promoter group pledging: VERIFY — not disclosed in the material used.
  • Index/benchmark effect: an expected ₹10 lakh Cr company will likely be added to NSE Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex post-listing (eligibility windows VERIFY) — a mechanical passive-inflow tailwind, not a thesis.

3. Growth

Section 3 — The View (plain English)
  • Revenue growth: FY24 ₹1,09,558 Cr → FY25 ₹1,28,218 Cr (+17.0%) → FY26 ₹1,46,885 Cr (+14.6%); two-year CAGR 18.6% (Calc). Q1 FY27 ₹39,173 Cr, +11.8% YoY — growth is decelerating but still double-digit.IMPORTANT
  • The growth engine has three legs: (1) subscriber additions (533.3 m at Jun-2026, +8.9 m / +1.7% QoQ), (2) ARPU (₹206.2 → ₹214 → ₹215.6 across FY25→FY26→Q1 FY27 — only +0.7% QoQ, Calc), (3) new lines of business — fixed broadband (28.6 m), 5G (285 m subscribers, free so far), JioAirFiber, JioHotstar, JioPay, enterprise/cloud (JioCloud, JioBridge) and the Jio-BP fuel JV.
  • Structural positives: ~60% share of India's wireless data traffic, 42.6% of fixed broadband, 50% of wireless broadband (RHP); data traffic itself keeps compounding with India's usage; every 1% of paid-5G conversion and each tariff round flows straight to ARPU.
  • What could break it: free 5G for 285 m subs dilutes ARPU until monetisation starts; tariff increases are discretionary (Airtel/Vi set the pace) and the DRHP itself flags a possible stall in tariff growth; competition from Airtel 5G and Vi's ₹20,000 Cr equity infusion keeps pricing power in check; net adds are modest vs rivals' churn plays.IMPORTANT
  • What must be true for the growth case to hold (watch these): ARPU ≥ ₹215 by Q2 FY27 · subscriber net adds staying positive · paid-5G rollout ≥ 2027 (management guide, from Nomura note) · fixed-broadband gross adds staying above ~1.5 m/quarter · related-party distributor revenue not rising as a share of revenue without an ARPU reason.IMPORTANT

4. Profitability & Efficiency

Section 4 — The View (plain English)
  • Margins are world-class: FY26 EBITDA margin 51.9% (FY25 ~50.4%, Calc) rising to 53.3% in Q1 FY27; net margin ~20.5% in FY26 (FY25 20.4%, FY24 19.6% — steady climb). Telecom with 51.9% EBITDA margins and 20.5% net margins is a structurally profitable utility-plus-data business.IMPORTANT
  • Profit trajectory: PAT ₹21,422 Cr (FY24) → ₹26,109 Cr (FY25, +21.9%) → ₹30,049 Cr (FY26, +15.1%) → ₹7,764 Cr (Q1 FY27, +9.2% YoY). Growth is good but slowing at the PAT line — partly the ₹1,840.5 Cr one-off already in FY26, partly competitive capex and free-5G costs.
