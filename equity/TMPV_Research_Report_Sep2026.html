4. Profitability & Efficiency

Section 4 — The View (plain English)
  • The operating engine weakened in the restructuring year: OPM was 13% in FY24 and 13% in FY25, then 6% in FY26 — and 6% again in the Jun-2026 quarter. Half the operating margin disappeared and has not come back.IMPORTANT
  • ROCE fell 19% → 16% → 3% across FY24–FY26 (2.73% TTM). A company earning 3% on capital employed against a 10–12% cost of capital is destroying value at the operating level — that is why the stock trades below book.IMPORTANT
  • Net margin and ROE are distorted: FY26 net margin of ~24.6% and ROE 75.7% are artefacts of the ₹86,291 Cr demerger gain sitting in other income. Ex-one-off, FY26 pre-tax profit is roughly −₹3,892 Cr.IMPORTANT
  • The genuine improvement is the interest line: ₹7,594 Cr (FY24) → ₹3,901 Cr (FY25) → ₹2,827 Cr (FY26), down 63% in two years — deleveraging is flowing straight to the P&L, giving roughly 6.6× interest cover on FY26 operating profit.
  • This is a working-capital-negative model: cash conversion cycle of −45 / −48 / −69 / −59 days and working-capital days of −63 to −81 — suppliers and dealers fund the inventory, which is structurally favourable for an OEM.
  • Signature test (Revenue up? Margin up? ROCE up?): ✗ / ✗ / ✗ — all three legs fail. This section scores 4.0/10.
