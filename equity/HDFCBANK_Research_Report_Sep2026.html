4. Profitability & Efficiency

Section 4 — The View (plain English)
  • Read this first — the bank-format trap: Screener computes Financing Profit = Revenue − Interest − Expenses. For HDFC that line is negative in every post-merger quarter (−₹2,066 Cr to −₹27,196 Cr) and −₹40,511 Cr in FY26, because ₹1.85 lakh Cr of interest plus ₹2.04 lakh Cr of expenses is deducted from the revenue line. Headline PBT = Financing Profit + Other Income — so the bank is not losing money; the export simply splits a bank's income and costs differently than a factory's P&L.IMPORTANT
  • Plain English: do not read the −10% to −34% "Financing Margin %" as an operating margin. A bank's real economics live in the spread between interest earned and interest paid, plus fee income — all of which this export spreads across Revenue / Interest / Other Income. Judged properly, FY26 net margin is 22.7% (₹79,219 Cr on ₹3,48,615 Cr).IMPORTANT
  • Earnings-quality flag: TTM other income ₹1,43,700 Cr = 41% of TTM revenue and 133% of TTM PBT — i.e. every rupee of pre-tax profit (and more) sits in the Other Income column while the financing line reads negative. Merger-related, fair-value and fee items post-1-Jul-2024 must be separated out → VERIFY from the annual report.IMPORTANT
  • Returns are good but drifting: ROE 17% (FY24) → 14% (FY25) → 14% (FY26), currently 13.6%; ROCE 7.02% (for a bank, ROCE is not very meaningful — interest is a raw-material cost, not capital).
  • The rest is clean: tax rate steady at 22–25%, dividend payout rising to 31% in FY26, EPS ₹49.39, and Screener's "low interest coverage ratio" con reflects interest-expense mechanics of the bank format rather than distress (interest of ₹1.85 lakh Cr is matched against a far larger interest-earning book → VERIFY against reported NIM).
